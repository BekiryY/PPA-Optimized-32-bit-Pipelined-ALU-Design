COLOR Convention of nets and instances for pdf files:

------- COLOR --------- MEANING -------
---------------------------------------
	YELLOW		clk
	ORANGE		reset_n
	CYAN		reset_n
	RED		CMD (opcode)
	RED		control related modules
	GREEN 		idle mode selection signals
	LIME		low_power-only modules
	LIME 		low_power mode selection signal
	BLUE		A & B inputs (operands)
	BLUE		High performance modules
	PURPLE 		output signals
